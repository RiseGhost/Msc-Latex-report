\chapter{Abstract}
\label{chap:abstract}

This paper presents the development of an innovative strategic management and simulation video game for technological infrastructures, combining the complexity and technical rigor of the computing ecosystem with a highly engaging gameplay experience. Assuming the role of a data center manager, the player is challenged to ensure the economic viability of their operation through financial resource optimization, power allocation, and the selection of real hardware components.

Developed in the Unity engine and paired with solid game design methodologies, such as the \ac{MDA}, Flow, and Hook Model frameworks, the product stands out by transforming technical knowledge into essential progression tools. The inclusion of dynamic combat mechanics against cyber threats and mini-games based on virtual terminals (WordRush) breaks the monotony of economic simulation, guaranteeing a continuous pace of excitement and player retention.

Tested and evaluated with real users, the video game demonstrated high potential for indirectly conveying advanced \ac{IT} and cybersecurity concepts, offering an educational entertainment solution ready to capture the interest of strategy, simulation, and technology enthusiasts.
